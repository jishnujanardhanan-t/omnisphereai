# OmniSphereAI - Project Vision

## Overview

OmniSphereAI is an AI-powered Salesforce intelligence platform designed to help administrators, developers, consultants, and business teams better understand, optimize, and automate their Salesforce environments.

The platform combines Salesforce data, metadata, and AI capabilities to provide actionable insights, automation recommendations, and intelligent assistance.

---

## Problem Statement

Salesforce organizations often become complex over time.

Common challenges include:

* Difficult-to-maintain automation
* Unused fields and objects
* Technical debt
* Poor documentation
* Slow troubleshooting
* Limited visibility into org health

Organizations need a simpler way to understand and optimize their Salesforce environments.

---

## Solution

OmniSphereAI provides an intelligent layer on top of Salesforce.

The platform analyzes Salesforce metadata, configuration, and business processes to generate insights and recommendations.

Users can interact with the platform using natural language and receive AI-powered guidance.
